Terms

The deal, in plain English.

Subscriptions renew every month until you cancel. Canceling takes one email. There’s no free trial.

Last updated November 18, 2026.

Who you’re dealing with

Virginia Bill Watch LLC, a Virginia limited liability company, 1201 Sycamore Sq #1313, Midlothian, VA 23113, rich@vabillwatch.com. By subscribing or ordering a scorecard, you agree to these terms on behalf of your organization.

What you get

A plain-English report every morning of the Virginia General Assembly session, compiled from the Commonwealth’s own legislative record and filtered against the issues your organization cares about. What each plan includes is listed on the pricing page. Custom legislator scorecards are one-time projects, scoped and priced in writing before we start.

Billing and renewal

  • Monthly plans are billed at the start of each month and renew automatically every month until you cancel.
  • Annual plans are paid up front for twelve months at the price of eleven, and renew automatically for another year unless you cancel before the renewal date.
  • No free trial. Your first charge happens when you sign up.
  • Prices are the ones published on the pricing page when you sign up. If we change a price, we’ll email you at least 30 days before it applies to you.

How to cancel

Email rich@vabillwatch.com with “cancel” and your organization’s name. That’s it. No phone call, no form, no one trying to talk you out of it. We’ll confirm by email.

Canceling stops the next charge. You keep getting reports through the end of the period you’ve already paid for. We don’t refund partial months.

What we are, and what we aren’t

We provide the data. No politics, right or left. We’re not lobbyists, we’re not lawyers, and nothing we send is legal advice or a recommendation on how to vote or what to support.

We read every number back against the live record before it goes to you, but the official record from the Virginia Legislative Information System is the final word. If we get something wrong, tell us and we’ll correct it.

We use AI to analyze the legislative record. How we handle information is on our privacy page.

Sharing the reports

Your reports are for your organization. Share them with your staff, your board, and your members. Don’t resell them or republish them as your own product.

Your information

The issues you tell us to watch, and anything else you share about your organization’s plans, stay between us. We don’t share them with anyone.

Limits on our liability

The service is provided as is. If something goes wrong, the most we’re responsible for is what you paid us in the twelve months before the problem. We’re not responsible for indirect losses, like a decision your organization made based on a report.

The legal fine print

These terms are governed by Virginia law. Any dispute goes to the courts of Chesterfield County, Virginia. If we change these terms, we’ll update this page and the date at the top, and email current subscribers about any change that affects them.
