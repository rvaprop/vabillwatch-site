Privacy

What we collect, and why.

Short version: if you came here from one of our emails, we can see that you visited and which pages you read. That’s the only tracking on this site. No ads, no analytics company, nothing sold.

Last updated November 18, 2026.

Who we are

Virginia Bill Watch LLC, a Virginia limited liability company, 1201 Sycamore Sq #1313, Midlothian, VA 23113. Questions about anything on this page go to rich@vabillwatch.com, and a real person answers.

If you came here from one of our emails

Every email we send has a link with a short six-character code on the end, like ?v=3f9a1c. That code is unique to you. We made it, and we know whose email it went into.

When you open this site through that link, your browser tells our server four things: the code, the page you’re looking at, the time, and what kind of browser you’re using. Because the code is yours, that tells us by name that you visited and what you read. We use it to know whether our email was useful and when to follow up. If you click a button to book a call, we note that too.

We don’t set a cookie. The code is held in your browser only until you close the tab. We don’t record your IP address. If you’d rather we didn’t see your visit, delete everything from ?v= onward in the address bar, or type vabillwatch.com in yourself. Without a code, nothing is recorded.

If you didn’t come from an email

We don’t record your visit at all. There’s no analytics tool, no ad pixel, no Facebook or Google tracking code on this site.

How we found you, if we wrote to you

We write to people who run Virginia organizations that work on state legislation. Before we write, we read what you and your organization have published publicly: your website, your public posts and statements, and the legislative record. We use that to write an email that’s actually about your work. We keep your name, your role, your organization, your work email address, and notes on that public material.

If you book a call

The booking page asks for your name, your email, an optional note, and it reads your time zone from your browser so the times show correctly. We use that to hold the time, send you a confirmation, send reminder emails before the call, and let you cancel. Calls run on Google Meet, so Google handles the call itself.

How we use AI

We use AI to read and analyze the public legislative record. We also use AI to write our outreach emails, which means the AI tool sees the public information about you described above. Those emails are sent on a schedule. When you reply, Rich reads it and answers you himself. If he hasn’t answered within a day, you may get an answer that AI drafted for him. We don’t use AI to make any decision about you.

Who else touches your information

  • GitHub hosts this website. Like any web host, its servers see standard request information when you load a page.
  • Google supplies the fonts this site uses, so your browser fetches them from Google. Google Workspace also runs our email, and Google Meet runs our calls.
  • The AI provider we use for drafting, as described above.

That’s the list. We don’t sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone, and we don’t share it with any campaign, party, or lobbyist.

Payments

We don’t take card payments on this site yet. When we do, a payment processor will handle your card details and we’ll never see your full card number. We’ll update this page before that starts.

Your choices

  • Stop our emails. Reply “no thanks” to any of them. You’re added to our do-not-contact list and you won’t hear from us again.
  • See what we have. Email us and we’ll send you what we hold about you.
  • Delete it. Email us and we’ll delete it. The one thing we keep is your email address on the do-not-contact list, because that’s the only way to make sure we never write to you again.

How long we keep it

We keep prospect notes, visit records, and booking records while we’re in touch with you or doing business with you, and delete them when you ask. The do-not-contact list is kept permanently.

Children

This site is for organizations and the people who run them. It isn’t meant for children, and we don’t knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
