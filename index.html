One bill, thirteen votes

What that looks like for a single bill.

HB888 changed what localities can require for off-street parking. It became law in 2026. Along the way it was voted on thirteen separate times. The floor votes were not close. The vote that decided it was.

HB888 (2026) · Minimum off-street parking requirements Chief patron: Irene Shin · Became law, Chapter 1075
Jan 30 House Subcommittee #1 Recommends reporting with substitute 6-2
Jan 30 House Counties, Cities and Towns Reported with substitute 15-6
Feb 5 House floor Read third time and passed House 61-37
Mar 2 Senate Local Government Decided it Reported with substitute 8-7
Mar 9 Senate floor Passed Senate with substitute 21-19
Mar 10 House floor Senate substitute agreed to by House 62-37
Apr 22 Both chambers Concurred in the Governor’s recommendation 62-38 · 21-18

Eight senators to seven, on a Monday in March, decided a law that applies to every parking lot in the Commonwealth. That committee vote is public. It is also the kind of thing nobody tells you about until the bill is already law. Source: Virginia Legislative Information System, the state’s own feed.
